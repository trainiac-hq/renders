## Data, renders and docs

The data is the measured geometry and livery colours. The measurements
were taken from published drawings and photographs. Liveries describe the
operators' paint schemes.

## Logos and wordmarks

The files in `logos/` are not covered by this repository's licences. They
are the train operators' trademarks and belong to them. They are here only
so the renders can show the trains as they really run. Each file's source
and copyright status (public domain, CC, or non-free "fair use" on English
Wikipedia) is in `logos/SOURCES.md`. Trademark rights apply whatever a
file's copyright status.

The renders show these logos as the trains carry them. The licence on a
render covers the render, not the logos in it.

## Reference photos

Each photo in `classes/<code>/refs/` keeps its own licence, listed with
its author and source in `classes/<code>/SOURCES.md`. Only freely
licensed photos (CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, public domain) are stored. CC BY
and CC BY-SA need the attribution those files give, and CC BY-SA photos
can't be relicensed. Others are listed by URL only. The crops in
`classes/<code>/crops/` are cut from these photos and keep their licences
and attribution in the same way.
