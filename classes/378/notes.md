Doubts, said plainly: units last photographed before 2022 may have been
refreshed since (earlier notes cite a refresh programme since 2018,
moving to Ilford in October 2026), so the true current share of refreshed
units is higher than 11/57; the 2022-26 subset is the better estimate (26 %).
378201 was refreshed in May 2022 (Kew Gardens photo) yet shows the yellow cab

reading for the small or night photos (378151, 378153, 378211, 378212) was
checked on larger crops. The two all-over wraps (378138, 378205) keep the
yellow cab top, so they count as O for the face.

## What differs from the 377 body

- **Car lengths:** DMOS 19.995 m, MOS 19.66 m (published); the 377's cab is
  20.20 m with a gangway, 19.99 m intermediate.
- **Face:** no gangway. A flat upright face with a central detrainment door
  (black upper half, yellow lower, thick dark-grey rubber seal round it from
  above the cap line to the blue band's bottom), two screens, angular
  quadrilateral lamp bezels (head outboard and higher, tail inboard and
  lower: same order as the 377), a round lamp above the left cluster only,
  the number on the right only, a D-shaped marker on the dome, and a blue
  band and skirt with a coupler recess under an orange waist line.
- **Doors:** 1.5 m opening published, 1.55 m aperture measured (the 377
  spec's 1.76 m is its outer door frame, not the opening). First passenger door centre 5.70 m from the
  nose (377 5.58), second 14.82 (377 14.83): the 377 numbers are accepted
  within the photo scatter (+/-0.16 m). Cab door front edge 1.31 m (377 0.97
  m): the 378's yellow nose module is longer.
- **Windows:** band 1.75-2.865 m (377 1.80-2.87); between the cab door and
  door 1 one wide window and one narrow, and between the doors a
  narrow-wide-wide-wide-narrow pattern (p208), not the 377's four equal
  windows.
- **Heights:** cantrail line 3.28 m, roof 3.774 m, body bottom 0.95 m (from
  the 377-grid diagram; same family).

## What the current model (`class378` in `classes/electrostar.ts`) gets wrong

1. Face: one wide screen and no door; the train has two screens and a
   central detrainment door (every photo; f-door-224).
2. Yellow panel runs to the coupler (`panel: box(0, 0.4, 1, 4.2)`): the
   yellow stops at an orange waist line (1.07-1.24 m) above a blue band and
   blue skirt (f-lower-224).
3. Lamps: rounded-rectangle pairs; the train has angular bezels with the
   headlamp outboard and higher, plus a round lamp above the left cluster
   and a D-shaped dome marker.
4. No number; the original livery has "378 2xx" in dark blue on the right.
5. Cab length 20.0 (published 19.995: fine); cab door at 1.4 m, 0.6 m wide:
   measured front edge 1.31, width ~0.74 (centre 1.67).
6. Passenger doors 1.6 m at 5.6/14.75 (cab) and 4.9/14.75 (next): measured
   1.55 at 5.70/14.82 (cab) and 5.26/14.40 (next, low confidence).
7. Windows drawn as three continuous strips; the train has separate
   windows (narrow/wide pattern) in a black band.
8. Bogies 2.95/17.12 (cab), 2.9/16.8 (next): published centres 14.173 m
   give 2.91/17.08 and 2.74/16.92.
9. `liveries/LO.ts` `default` is fine for the doors (`#EE7623` vs sampled
   `#FC7E15`): the leaves are solid orange (s-door-tmd07 square-on, settled;
   the "silver with orange edges" on p224 were OPEN doors showing the cream
   surround). The default has no yellow cab-side wrap and no face bands.
10. Wheelbase 2.6 m matches the published value (electrostarBase).

## Open questions

- Car 2 door positions: p204 gives 5.69 m from the front and 4.83 m from
  the rear; I used the symmetric mean 5.26 from each end. A side-on of an
  MOS would settle it.
- Car 2 windows are inferred from the cab car pattern, not measured.
- The refreshed corner-stripe and lower-face shapes are approximate
  (+/-0.1 m): measured from 1600 px photos and scaled to p224's face; the
  refreshed camera match should fix them.
- Nose profile not measured (no 2024-26 side-on of the nose); the spec uses
  the 377 profile without the gangway valance.
- `face.lamps[1]` uses a unit kind "spot" (the round lamp-plus-sensor) and
  `frontDoor.handle`, `screens[].glass`/`dest`: keys beyond the schema, each
  described in `trace`.

## Elevation and pairs

`elevation.py --livery LO` went from 7/30 to 29/30 on the existing render.
Most of the failures were the checks themselves:

- **The window runs merged.** Glass (#14181A) and the window band (#2A2E33)
  are 25 apart, inside the default colour tolerance of 40, so every window
  ran through the band and its neighbours. `colourTol` is now 12 for both.
- **Door 1 checks hit the leaf joint and the panes.** The full-height
  column was at 5.70, the centre seal. It moved to 5.58, between the leaf
  window and the seal. The aperture run at 2.3 m crossed both leaf windows.
  It moved to 2.9 m, above them, and measures each leaf from its own middle.
- **Yellow seam at 2.0 m.** Walking back from the nose, the check took the
  cab door's front grab rail (#DADBDD, sitting on the yellow at 1.21-1.24)
  for the white bodyside. At 2.0 m the cab door window also starts right at
  the seam, so no white follows the yellow there at all. The check moved to
  1.5 m (p208 gives 1.35 from 2.01 m down to 1.45) and holds white to 6.

One finding was a model error: `heights.window` held the window band
(p208: 1.75-2.865), but the build treats it as the glass and paints the
band 0.1 m round it. The band came out 1.65-2.965. On s-windows-208 the
glass sits inside the band, 0.08 m from the top and 0.10 m from the bottom,
so `heights.window` is now the glass, 1.85-2.765. That draws the measured
band. This also raises the tumblehome's start (`shell.ts` waist) by
0.1 m. The window-band-at-a-pillar check stays failing until 378-LO is
re-rendered.

Not changed, for a decision:

- Cab door glass: the trace gives 1.52-1.96 m with `panes` [-0.15, 0.29],
  but the render puts it at 1.37-1.81. `layout.ts` places a single leaf's
  pane at x-b..x-a, which is the reverse of the `panes` doc comment
  ("negative is ahead"). The 377 spec was written to match the code and the
  357 spec to match the doc. The render does look like p208, with the
  window close behind the seam, so the photo reading should be checked too.
- The render puts a grey grab rail on the yellow ahead of the cab door
  seam. p208 shows none there.

Pairs: 14 judged and 4 refreshed ones skipped (their livery is not drawn).
One line each is in checklist.md. The gaps worth fixing are the missing
dome marker, the front door seal (drawn in yellow, not dark grey), the door
zigzag strips and cream surround (`doorStripes` is in the spec but not
passed by `liveries/LO.ts`), the tail lens colour, and the plain coupler.
