# Class 221 Super Voyager, CrossCountry: detail checklist

From Commons photos: 221 113 at Southampton Central, June 2026 (front
three-quarter, very close), 221 122 at Dore and Totley, January 2025
(front three-quarter, headlamps lit). Both in maroon and silver. The render
shares the 220's nose and livery, so most items repeat the 220 list.

Before a shape item is ticked, the render and the photo are compared from
the same view, side by side at the same scale, and the difference checked
is written down. Left and right are judged against the unmirrored photo.
