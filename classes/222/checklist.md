# Class 222 Meridian, East Midlands Railway: detail checklist

From Commons photos: 222 003 at Leicester, August 2025 (front three-quarter,
close, tail lamps lit), 222 006 at Market Harborough, September 2025 (front
three-quarter). Both in EMR purple.

Before a shape item is ticked, the render and the photo are compared from
the same view, side by side at the same scale, and the difference checked
is written down. Left and right are judged against the unmirrored photo.
