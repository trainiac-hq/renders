# Class 196 Civity, West Midlands Railway: detail checklist

From Commons photos: 196 111 at Wolverhampton, October 2025 (near head-on,
headlamps lit), 196 002 at Coventry Arena, March 2025 (front three-quarter,
close). Both in WMR orange and purple. Compared against
out/renders/196-LM-hero.png, -plate.png and -ids.png (re-rendered 2026-10-04
22:48).

Before a shape item is ticked, the render and the photo are compared from
the same view, side by side at the same scale, and the difference checked
is written down. Left and right are judged against the unmirrored photo.
