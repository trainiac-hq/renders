# Class 810 Aurora, East Midlands Railway: detail checklist

From Commons photos: an 810 at Kilby Bridge, April 2025 (front
three-quarter, headlamps lit), an 810 at Newark, and EMR's Aurora publicity
photo. The Aurora nose is drawn more simply than the 800's, from the side
only. Compared with our render 810-EM (hero and plate). Ticked only when a
crop of the render beside the same area of a photo matches in shape,
position, size and material.

Before a shape item is ticked, the render and the photo are compared from
the same view, side by side at the same scale, and the difference checked
is written down. Left and right are judged against the unmirrored photo.

Status: `todo`, `partial`, `done`.
