for the lamps; 153362 + 153368 at Cardiff Central (front three-quarter and

(large cab); the small added cab is not covered here. Each item is ticked
only when a crop of the render beside the same area of a photo matches in
shape, position, size and material.

the same view, side by side at the same scale, and the difference checked
is written down. Left and right are judged against the unmirrored photo.
