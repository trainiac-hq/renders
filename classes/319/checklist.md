# Class 319, Northern: detail checklist

From Commons photos: 319 367 at Liverpool Lime Street, November 2023 (front
three-quarter, close, tail lamps lit), 319 386 at Manchester Piccadilly,
February 2022 (front three-quarter). Northern's 319s are the last in
service; no 2024-2026 photo on Commons shows the cab as well as these.

Before a shape item is ticked, the render and the photo are compared from
the same view, side by side at the same scale, and the difference checked
is written down. Left and right are judged against the unmirrored photo.

Status: `todo`, `partial`, `done`.
