three-quarter, close), 805 013 at Chester, October 2025 (front
three-quarter, tail lamps lit). The Evero nose is drawn more simply than the
800's, from the side only. Compared with our render 805-VT (hero and plate).

matches in shape, position, size and material.

Before a shape item is ticked, the render and the photo are compared from
the same view, side by side at the same scale, and the difference checked
is written down. Left and right are judged against the unmirrored photo.
