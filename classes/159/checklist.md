# Class 159, South Western Railway: detail checklist

From Commons photos: 159006 (front three-quarter, close, current SWR navy and
silver livery; read for the lamps), 159009 at Exeter St Davids (three-quarter,
same livery, newly refurbished). Also in refs/: 159103 at Salisbury and 159104
at Westbury in the earlier white/red SWR livery, kept for shape only. The 159
shares the Class 158 body, so the 158 checklist covers the cab structure in
more depth. Each item is ticked only when a crop of the render beside the same
area of a photo matches in shape, position, size and material.

Before a shape item is ticked, the render and the photo are compared from
the same view, side by side at the same scale, and the difference checked
is written down. Left and right are judged against the unmirrored photo.
