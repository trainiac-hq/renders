# Class 220 Voyager, CrossCountry: detail checklist

From Commons photos: 220 022 at Southampton Central, June 2026 (front
three-quarter, close, maroon and silver), and 220 at Reading, April 2026
(front three-quarter, in the red refresh with the cream X that is spreading
across the fleet). The render uses the maroon and silver scheme, so shape
and livery items are judged against 220 022 unless stated.

Before a shape item is ticked, the render and the photo are compared from
the same view, side by side at the same scale, and the difference checked
is written down. Left and right are judged against the unmirrored photo.
