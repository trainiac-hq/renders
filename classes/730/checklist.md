# Class 730 Aventra, West Midlands Trains (LM): detail checklist

Our render 730-LM is in London Northwestern green, so it is compared with
the LNR photos: 730 223 at Crewe, 2026 (front three-quarter, very close),
730 212 (three-quarter). West Midlands Railway photos 730 031 at Birmingham
New Street, April 2026 and 730 035 are also in refs/ for the 730/0 sets in
WMR gold and purple, which the render does not show. Ticked only when a
crop of the render beside the same area of a photo matches in shape,
position, size and material.

Before a shape item is ticked, the render and the photo are compared from
the same view, side by side at the same scale, and the difference checked
is written down. Left and right are judged against the unmirrored photo.

Status: `todo`, `partial`, `done`.
